Master Printmaking Pillar

Haute Hot Foil Stamping & Antique Letterpress

The pinnacle of luxury wedding stationery: sculpted metal dies that permanently press metallic luster and deep dimensional relief into museum-grade cotton rag boards.

Precision Craftsmanship

The Alchemy of Heat, Pressure & Heavy Cotton

Unlike commercial digital printing which sprays ink onto the surface of paper, our process uses CNC-engraved solid magnesium and brass dies. Heated to precisely 135°C (275°F), the die activates the thermal adhesive on the reverse of the foil while several tons of mechanical pressure press the pigment deep into the fibers.

  • Deboss Depth: 0.35mm tactile relief without bruising or cracking the backside of duplexed boards.
  • Archival Integrity: Foils do not tarnish, oxidize, or rub off over centuries of heirloom storage.
  • Multi-Pass Capability: Combine deep matte letterpress ink text with gilded names in a single master card.
