Our Story & Heritage

Preserving the Sacred Art of Slow Stationery

Founded on the conviction that a wedding invitation is not merely paper, but the first physical portal into the sacred atmosphere of your union.

Artisanal Philosophy

An Atelier Born From Passion for Tactile Beauty

In a digital world dominated by fleeting text messages, we celebrate paper that carries gravity, depth, and permanence.

Every envelope folded at Aura & Vellum is crafted from sustainable 100% recycled cotton remnants from the textile trade. No virgin trees are felled; instead, we partner with heritage Italian and French paper mills that have pulverized cotton into velvety, feather-edged sheets since the 18th century.

Our printing is entirely mechanical and artisanal. When an engraved brass die presses into cotton rag with 4,000 pounds of pressure, the indentation becomes permanent, casting microscopic shadows that invite touch.

Our Milestones

Two Decades of Devotion to the Craft

2008

The First Press

Founder Genevieve Beauchamp rescues and restores a 1904 Heidelberg platen press from a defunct Florentine bookbinder.

2014

Deckle Edge Mill Alliance

We establish an exclusive partnership with an Amalfi Coast watermill to produce custom-watermarked 600gsm cotton boards.

2019

SoHo Flagship Lounge

Opening of our private client consultation salon on Broome Street in Manhattan, hosting couples from over 32 countries.

2026

Interactive Digital Atelier

Launch of our interactive 3D suite customizer and digital client proofing portal, bringing bespoke couture to brides globally.
